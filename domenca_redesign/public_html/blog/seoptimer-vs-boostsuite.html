Which SEO tool should your agency choose in 2026?

Where SEOptimer Wins

  • Mature product — been around longer, more established
  • White-label reports — beautiful PDFs with custom branding
  • Embeddable widget — agencies can put audit forms on their own websites
  • SEO Crawler — deep technical crawling of entire sites
  • Keyword tracking — monitor rankings over time
  • Backlink analysis — understand link profiles

Where BoostSuite Wins

  • All-in-one value — 4 tools instead of just SEO auditing
  • GEO Check — unique AI visibility analysis (how ChatGPT/Gemini recommend you)
  • Ad Copy Generator — create Google/Meta ad copy with AI
  • Listing Optimizer — optimize Etsy/Amazon/Google Business listings
  • Price — €19/mo gets you 4 tools vs SEOptimer's $29/mo for 1
  • No registration — use all tools without creating an account

The Real Difference

SEOptimer is a dedicated SEO audit tool that does one thing very well. If you only need SEO auditing and white-label reports, it's the better choice.

BoostSuite is a marketing toolkit that combines SEO auditing with 3 other tools agencies actually need. If you're paying for separate SEO, ad copy, and listing tools, BoostSuite replaces all of them.

Bottom line: Choose SEOptimer if you specifically need an embeddable audit widget or advanced technical crawling. Choose BoostSuite if you want to replace 3-4 separate subscriptions with one affordable tool.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is SEOptimer better than BoostSuite?
It depends on your needs. SEOptimer is better for pure SEO auditing and white-label reports. BoostSuite is better value if you need multiple marketing tools.

Can I use BoostSuite for client work?
Yes! The Agency plan (€49/mo) includes white-label reports and API access for client projects.

Does BoostSuite have a crawler like Screaming Frog?
Not yet. BoostSuite focuses on AI-powered auditing rather than deep technical crawling. For technical SEO crawling, you'd still want Screaming Frog alongside it.

What is GEO and why does it matter?
GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) checks how visible your business is in AI chatbot responses. As more people use ChatGPT and Gemini for search, GEO visibility becomes critical.
