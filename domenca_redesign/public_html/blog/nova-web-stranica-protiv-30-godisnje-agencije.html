Može li nova web stranica pobijediti 30-godišnju agenciju? Iskren odgovor

Imate novu web stranicu. Vaš konkurent ima 30 godina povijesti. Je li uopće moguće pobijediti ih? Odgovor ovisi o kanalu.

In the previous article we talked about 5 SEO factors you can never beat. Now let's look at the other side of the coin:

Is there a channel where a new domain can beat a 30-year-old agency?

The answer: Yes, but only under certain conditions.

First: the reality of SEO

❌ Traditional SEO (Google):

A 30-year-old agency has:

  • 19+ years of backlinks
  • Thousands of content pages
  • Google Partner, Meta Certified certifications
  • Big-name client references (Sberbank, government, Hervis)
  • A brand people know

You can't catch up to this in a year. Maybe not even in five years.

7 ways you can (and can't) beat the competition

1 GEO optimization — your BIGGEST opportunity

✅ Possible: YES

GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) optimizes for AI search — ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity.

Why it works for new domains:

  • AI doesn't look at domain age — it looks at content quality
  • AI doesn't look at backlinks the same way Google does
  • AI doesn't know brands — it cites what it finds
  • GEO isn't 20 years old — we're all at the beginning
  • You already have a 3-6 month head start over older agencies

2 Long-tail keywords — small wins

✅ Possible: YES (partially)

Long-tail keywords are specific phrases with less competition.

KeywordCompetition
"web agency"🔴 Very high
"web agency Ljubljana"🟡 High
"website for restaurants Slovenia"🟢 Low
"AI optimization for small businesses"🟢 Very low

3 Lokalni SEO — your advantage

✅ Possible: YES

Google My Business, local citations, customer reviews — things you can improve quickly.

4 Paid advertising — instant visibility

✅ Possible: YES

Google Ads and Meta Ads bypass SEO entirely. Pay and you're at the top.

5 Social media — traffic without Google

✅ Possible: YES

LinkedIn, Instagram, Facebook — traffic that doesn't come from Google.

6 Extreme specialization

✅ Possible: YES

Instead of "web agency for everyone," be "web agency for restaurants" or "AI chatbot for hotels."

7 Content quality — depth over breadth

⚠️ Possible: PARTIALLY

If you write an exceptionally deep guide (3000+ words, examples, data), Google may reward it — even on a new domain. But for competitive keywords, it's not enough.

Conclusion: where is your opportunity?

GEO is your biggest opportunity. But it's not the only one.

Recommended strategy for a new domain:

  1. GEO optimization — start now, today (you can start for free)
  2. Long-tail keywords — target specific, less competitive terms
  3. Lokalni SEO — Google My Business, reviews, local citations
  4. Content — write deep, not broad
  5. Social media — traffic without Google
  6. Paid ads — for instant visibility (if you have budget)

Realistic timeline:

  • GEO: 3-6 months for noticeable results
  • Long-tail SEO: 6-12 months
  • Lokalni SEO: 3-6 months
  • Traditional SEO (competitive keywords): 2-5 years

Frequently Asked Questions

Can a new domain reach page 1 of Google for competitive keywords?

Difficult, but possible — with a long-term strategy (2-5 years), quality content and backlinks. For long-tail keywords, you can succeed in 6-12 months.

How quickly do I see results from GEO optimization?

Usually 3-6 months. AI tools index content slower than Google, but competition is also lower.

Is it worth paying for Google Ads with a new domain?

Yes, for instant visibility. But it's not a long-term solution. Combine with SEO and GEO for lasting results.
