5 SEO factors you can never beat (and what to do instead)

Domain age, big client backlinks, a 30-year brand — things your competition can't catch up to in a year. But what CAN you do?

Have you ever looked at a competitor on Google and thought: "How am I supposed to beat this?"

Maybe their website is older than your company. Maybe they have big-name client references you can't get. Maybe people have known their brand for 30 years.

The truth: some things in SEO you simply cannot beat. Not in a year. Maybe not even in five years.

But that doesn't mean you're doomed. It means you need to play differently.

1 Domain age

This is the biggest and most painful truth in SEO.

Real-world example:

Art Design Ljubljana — domain since 1997. 29 years of history.

Red Orbit — domain since 2007. 19 years of history.

New agency — domain since 2023. 3 years.

Google trusts older domains. That's a fact.

Why? Because an older domain has:

  • More backlinks — 19 years means thousands of links from other sites
  • More history — Google sees the site has existed longer = more reliable
  • More content — older sites have more pages, articles, references
  • More trust — Google won't instantly rank new domains at the top

You can't "fake" domain age. You can't buy 20 years of history.

What can you do instead?

  • Start today — every day counts. Start now and you'll have 3 years of history in 3 years
  • Focus on GEO — AI tools (ChatGPT, Gemini) don't look at domain age. They look at content quality. This is where you can beat 30-year agencies
  • Buy an old domain — if you find an old domain with good authority, you can buy it and redirect

2 Big client backlinks

When your competitor has Slovenian Army, Sberbank, or Hervis in their portfolio, it's not just references — it's backlinks from high-authority domains.

Backlink fromPower (Domain Rating)
gov.si (Slovenian government)90+ 🔥

One backlink from gov.si is worth more than 100 backlinks from small sites. And you can't buy or build that overnight.

What can you do instead?

  • Guest posting — write for other blogs and earn backlinks
  • PR articles — publish in media (Siol, Finance, Dnevnik)
  • Directories — list in Slovenian business directories
  • Partnerships — collaborate with other companies and exchange backlinks
  • GEO — again: AI tools don't look at backlinks the same way

3 Brand awareness

People have known "Art Design" for 30 years. "Red Orbit" is synonymous with digital marketing in Slovenia.

This isn't just SEO — it's brand awareness. And it's the hardest thing to build.

When someone types "art design ljubljana" into Google, Google knows people are looking for that specific agency. And puts them first.

You might have a better website, better content, better offer — but if people don't know your name, they won't search for it.

What can you do instead?

  • Building brand ≠ Building SEO — focus on awareness, not just rankings
  • Social media — post regularly, build community
  • Local SEO — Google My Business, local references, customer reviews
  • AI visibility — when ChatGPT mentions you, people start searching your name

4 E-A-T: Experience, Authoritativeness, Trust

Google has a concept called E-A-T: Experience, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness.

  • Experience — do you have experience in this field?
  • Authoritativeness — do others treat you as an authority?
  • Trustworthiness — can people trust you?

A company with 29 years of experience, Google Partner and Meta Certified certifications, and clients like Sberbank — has E-A-T in the bag.

You? Maybe you have great products, but Google doesn't know that yet.

What can you do instead?

  • Get certifications — Google Analytics, Meta Blueprint, HubSpot
  • Write authoritative articles — show knowledge, not just sales
  • Get reviews — Google My Business, Trustpilot, LinkedIn recommendations
  • Case studies — publish your clients' results (with permission)

5 Industry certifications and partnerships

Google Partner, Meta Certified, Bing Accredited — these aren't just logos on a page. They're trust signals that Google considers.

When a competitor has 6 Google/Meta certifications, it means:

  • Google knows they're verified
  • Users see the logos and trust them
  • Ad platforms give them priority

You might have better results — but without certifications, Google has a harder time seeing that.

What can you do instead?

  • Get certifications — most are free or cheap
  • Specialize — instead of "everything under one roof," be the best at one thing
  • AI niche — you're the only one offering GEO optimization in Slovenia. That's your certification

The big twist: What CAN you beat?

AI visibility (GEO) is the only SEO channel where small businesses can beat big ones — RIGHT NOW.

Why? Because:

  • ChatGPT doesn't look at domain age — it looks at content quality
  • AI doesn't look at backlinks — it looks at relevance and structured data
  • AI doesn't know brands — it cites what it finds on the web
  • GEO isn't 20 years old — we're all at the beginning, and you already have a 3-month head start

Example: A 30-year agency has SEO you can't beat. But they don't have GEO optimization. You can have it today. And when ChatGPT recommends "the best agency for AI optimization in Slovenia" — it will cite YOU, not them.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I buy an old domain for better SEO?

Yes, you can. But be careful — only buy domains with clean history (no spam backlinks). And the redirect (301) must be set up correctly.

How long does it take for a new domain to gain Google's trust?

Usually 12-24 months for noticeable results. It can be accelerated with quality content and backlinks.

Is GEO really better than SEO?

No — both are needed. SEO is the foundation, GEO is the future. You get the best results with both strategies.

Can a small business beat a big agency on Google?

Difficult, but possible — with a long-term strategy, quality content, and specialization. In GEO optimization, you can even beat 30-year agencies, because it's a new channel.
