# 90-Day Growth Plan - Q2 2026

## Overview
This plan focuses on **user acquisition** for your 4 SaaS apps using a multi-channel approach. Goal: **100+ new Pro subscribers** across all apps by end of Q2.

---

## Phase 1: Foundation (Days 1-30)

### Week 1-2: Outreach Infrastructure
- [ ] Set up email tracking (Mailtrack, HubSpot, or similar)
- [ ] Build a simple CRM or Google Sheets tracker (Lead, Status, Contact Date, Response, Next Step)
- [ ] Create email templates for each ICP (see `Outreach_templates.md`)
- [ ] Set up LinkedIn profile optimization (headline: "Building AI tools for SEO, Etsy, Restaurants")
- [ ] Write 3 LinkedIn posts showcasing each app's value (with screenshots/results)

### Week 3-4: First Outreach Wave
- [ ] **Target**: 20 leads from `OSINT_leads_2026Q2.csv`
- [ ] Send 5 emails/day (personalized)
- [ ] Engage on LinkedIn (comment on their posts before DMing)
- [ ] Track open rates, replies, conversions
- [ ] **Goal**: 3-5 conversations, 1-2 Pro sign-ups

**KPIs**:
- 20 emails sent
- 30% open rate
- 10% reply rate
- 1-2 Pro sign-ups

---

## Phase 2: Scale (Days 31-60)

### Week 5-6: Content + SEO
- [ ] Publish 2 blog posts per app:
  - SEOBooster: "How to audit your website SEO in 60 seconds"
  - EtsyBooster: "Etsy SEO mistakes killing your sales"
  - MenuBoost: "Why AI menu descriptions boost restaurant bookings"
  - GEOBoost: "Is ChatGPT recommending your business?"
- [ ] Optimize landing pages for SEO (meta titles, descriptions, H1s)
- [ ] Submit to Product Hunt (pick 1 app to launch first)
- [ ] Post on Reddit (r/SEO, r/Etsy, r/restaurantowners) — value-first, not spammy

### Week 7-8: Partnerships + Affiliates
- [ ] Reach out to 5 agencies from the lead list with affiliate/partner offers
- [ ] Offer **20% commission** on referrals (tracked via Gumroad affiliate links)
- [ ] Create a simple "Partner Program" page on each app site
- [ ] Write case studies from early users (even if it's just 1-2 screenshots)

**KPIs**:
- 2 blog posts published
- 1 Product Hunt launch (goal: top 10 for the day)
- 2 affiliate partners onboarded
- 5-10 Pro sign-ups

---

## Phase 3: Acceleration (Days 61-90)

### Week 9-10: Paid Ads (Low Budget)
- [ ] Run **€50/week Google Ads** for high-intent keywords:
  - "free SEO audit tool"
  - "Etsy listing generator"
  - "restaurant menu generator"
  - "AI visibility checker"
- [ ] A/B test ad copy + landing pages
- [ ] Track conversions (free → Pro)

### Week 11-12: Community + PR
- [ ] Join 3-5 niche communities (Slack, Discord, Facebook groups for SEO pros, Etsy sellers, restaurant owners)
- [ ] Share tools in "tool Tuesday" threads or similar (no spam)
- [ ] Pitch 2-3 niche blogs/newsletters for features (e.g., "5 tools every Etsy seller needs")
- [ ] Reach out to micro-influencers (100-10K followers) in each niche for reviews/shoutouts

**KPIs**:
- €200 ad spend, 10+ conversions (Pro sign-ups)
- 2 blog features or newsletter mentions
- 3 community mentions/shares
- 10-15 Pro sign-ups

---

## End of 90 Days: Targets

| Metric | Target |
|--------|--------|
| **Total Pro Subscribers** | 30-50 across all apps |
| **Email List Growth** | 200+ free users |
| **Affiliate Partners** | 3-5 active partners |
| **Organic Traffic** | 1,000+ visits/month |
| **Product Hunt Launch** | Top 10 of the day |
| **Revenue** | €300-500 MRR |

---

## Weekly Habits (Ongoing)
- [ ] **Monday**: Plan outreach for the week (5-10 new leads)
- [ ] **Tuesday**: Content day (write 1 blog post or LinkedIn post)
- [ ] **Wednesday**: Outreach follow-ups + LinkedIn engagement
- [ ] **Thursday**: Product updates + user feedback review
- [ ] **Friday**: Metrics review + adjust strategy

---

## Tools to Use
- **Email**: Mailtrack, HubSpot, or Gmail + Google Sheets
- **CRM**: Notion, Airtable, or Google Sheets
- **SEO**: SEOBooster (dogfood your own tool!), Ahrefs, Ubersuggest
- **Social Scheduling**: Buffer, Hypefury
- **Ads**: Google Ads, Facebook Ads (low budget)
- **Analytics**: Google Analytics, Plausible

---

## Red Flags to Watch
- **Low open rates (<20%)**: Improve subject lines
- **High bounce rate on landing pages (>70%)**: Simplify CTA
- **No conversions after 2 weeks**: Revisit ICP or messaging
- **No affiliate interest**: Increase commission or add incentives

---

## Notes
- **Focus on one app per month** if spreading thin (Month 1: SEOBooster, Month 2: EtsyBooster, Month 3: MenuBoost/GEOBoost)
- **Double down on what works**: If Reddit drives traffic, do more Reddit. If email converts, send more emails.
- **Don't ignore existing users**: Send weekly tips, ask for feedback, offer referral bonuses.
