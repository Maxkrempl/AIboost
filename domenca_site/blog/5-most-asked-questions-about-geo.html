1. What is Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)?

GEO is the practice of optimizing your online presence so AI tools recommend you.

When someone asks ChatGPT, "What's the best web designer in Ljubljana?" or Perplexity, "Which restaurant management software should I use?" — the AI doesn't just list websites. It reads, analyzes, and recommends based on what it knows about your business.

GEO is about making sure that when AI reads about your industry, it knows you exist and considers you worth recommending.

This includes:

  • Structured data (schema markup) — giving AI a machine-readable map of your business
  • Citations and mentions — being referenced by authoritative sources AI trusts
  • Multi-platform presence — appearing across directories, review sites, Wikipedia, social media
  • Content that answers questions — not just marketing copy, but genuinely useful answers
  • Technical health — fast loading, mobile-friendly, clean HTML that AI can parse

Think of it this way: SEO got you listed in Google's index. GEO gets you recommended in AI's answers.

2. How is GEO different from SEO?

This is the question everyone asks, and for good reason — the two overlap but serve very different discovery channels.

SEO (Search Engine Optimization)

  • Goal: rank on page 1 of Google
  • Audience: Google's crawlers and human clickers
  • Key signals: keywords, backlinks, Core Web Vitals
  • Result: a list of blue links

GEO (Generative Engine Optimization)

  • Goal: be cited and recommended by AI
  • Audience: LLMs (ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Claude)
  • Key signals: structured data, brand mentions, authoritative citations, factual accuracy
  • Result: AI-generated recommendation or summary

The crucial difference: Google ranks pages. AI recommends businesses.

A user searching Google sees 10 blue links and picks one. A user asking ChatGPT gets one recommendation — and they follow it. There's no page 2 in AI search. You're either recommended or you don't exist.

This is why GEO matters: it's the difference between being one of ten options on Google and being the answer AI gives.

3. Will GEO replace SEO?

No. But SEO without GEO is increasingly incomplete.

Here's the reality most articles don't tell you:

✅ SEO fundamentals ARE GEO foundations. Fast loading, clean structure, quality content, mobile-friendly — these are non-negotiable for both. You can't do GEO on a broken website.

✅ Google still drives 80%+ of search traffic. Ignoring SEO for GEO would be like ignoring your storefront to build a billboard. You need both.

⚠️ AI search is growing fast. ChatGPT has 300M+ weekly users. Google AI Overviews appear on 30% of searches. This isn't a trend — it's a permanent shift in how people discover businesses.

❌ Businesses that ONLY do SEO will lose ground. Every month, more users get answers from AI instead of clicking blue links. If AI doesn't know you, those users never find you.

The businesses winning in 2026 are the ones doing both. They rank on Google and get recommended by AI. That's not a coincidence — it's strategy.

4. How do I optimize my website for GEO?

Practical steps you can take right now:

Step 1: Add structured data (schema markup)

This is the single most impactful GEO action. Schema tells AI exactly what your business does, where it's located, what services you offer, and what customers say. Without it, AI has to guess — and it often guesses wrong or skips you entirely.

Step 2: Get mentioned across platforms

AI doesn't just read your website. It cross-references what it finds across the internet. If your business appears on Google Business Profile, Yelp, industry directories, Wikipedia, and local news sites — AI trusts you more. A mention on a reputable site is worth more than a paragraph on your own.

Step 3: Create FAQ-rich content

AI tools answer questions. If your content directly answers the questions your customers ask, AI is more likely to cite you. Write content that answers "How much does X cost?" "What's the difference between X and Y?" "Is X right for my business?"

Step 4: Build a multi-platform presence

Your website alone isn't enough. AI models draw from Reddit, Quora, YouTube transcripts, social media, and dozens of other sources. The more places your brand appears with consistent, accurate information, the more likely AI is to recommend you.

Step 5: Keep your technical house in order

Fast loading, clean HTML, mobile-friendly, SSL certificate, working sitemap. AI crawlers have the same technical requirements as Google — arguably stricter, because they won't wait 5 seconds for your page to load.

5. How do I measure GEO performance?

This is where it gets tricky — there's no "GEO Analytics" dashboard yet. But you can measure it:

  • Direct test: Ask ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Gemini about your business or industry in your area. If you're not mentioned, AI doesn't know you exist.
  • AI visibility checkers: Tools like Semrush's AI Visibility Toolkit track where and how AI mentions your brand.
  • Referral traffic: Check if you're getting traffic from AI sources (referral data from ChatGPT, Perplexity, etc.).
  • Structured data validation: Use Google's Rich Results Test to verify your schema is being indexed correctly.
  • Brand mention monitoring: Track where your brand appears across the web — every mention is a potential citation AI can use.

The simplest test: open ChatGPT and ask it to recommend businesses like yours in your city. If you're not in the answer, you have a GEO problem.
