## For people who are allowed to taste.

We can boast of top-quality dishes, which certainly pamper the taste feels of each of our guests. The food we serve is made locally and always fresh, so every guest can easily feel like at home. The meat we serve is exclusively of Slovenian origin. Seasonal offerings also give guests an opportunity to experience seasons through the taste. The pleasant ambience and relaxation of the collective conjures up a very special atmosphere.

## [Parma restaurant](https://www.parma-restavracija.si/en/restaurant-parma/)

## The one and only in maribor

The [Parma Restaurant](https://www.parma-restavracija.si/en/restaurant-parma/) connects the former employees of the famous Italian restaurant, under the base of Pohorje, to which every one of you must have come at least once.

Our story ended there and gave us extra desire to continue the same story in a new place, new ambient and new energy.
