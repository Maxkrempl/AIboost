# Pricing hypotheses (two per app)

This document outlines two testable pricing hypotheses per app, aligned with Gumroad tier logic and pilot onboarding. Each hypothesis includes rationale, suggested price points, and expected metrics.

## EtsyBooster
- Hypothesis A: Free to start, paid upgrades for advanced features (e.g., batch listing optimization, multilingual bundles). Price: $9.99/mo starter, $29.99/mo growth.
- Hypothesis B: Pay-per-description bundles (e.g., 20 listings for $19, 100 for $89) to test usage-based pricing.

## SEOBooster
- Hypothesis A: Free audit with tiered ongoing monitoring. Price: $19.99/mo basic audit, $59.99/mo pro with ongoing optimization suggestions.
- Hypothesis B: Annual plan with a la carte audits; 2 audits included per year + monthly tune-up reports.

## MenuBoost
- Hypothesis A: Free trial with 20 descriptions; then $14.99/mo for 200 descriptions; add-ons for languages.
- Hypothesis B: Per-description pricing: $1.99 per description; discounts at higher volumes.

## GEOBoost
- Hypothesis A: Free audit + 1 AI visibility check; then $9.99/mo for ongoing visibility checks + site audit updates.
- Hypothesis B: Volume-based pricing for agencies; monthly retainer for ongoing monitoring and audits.
