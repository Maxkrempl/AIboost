# Landing briefs (one-page per app)

## EtsyBooster
- Target persona: Etsy sellers looking to save time and improve listing visibility.
- Message: AI-generated titles/descriptions/tags in under 10 seconds; free to try.
- Conversion focus: Start a free listing optimization now; highlight time savings and potential revenue uplift.
- KPIs: signups, listing optimizations completed, conversion rate to paid.

## SEOBooster
- Target persona: Small business owners needing quick, actionable SEO audits.
- Message: Fast, multi-language SEO health checks with concrete fixes; free audit.
- Conversion focus: Run a free audit; upsell ongoing monitoring.
- KPIs: audits run per week, conversion to paid, report downloads.

## MenuBoost
- Target persona: Restaurants and tourist farms needing compelling menus in multiple languages.
- Message: 10-second, multilingual, multi-style menu copy generation.
- Conversion focus: Try a free description; upsell bundles/descriptions.
- KPIs: descriptions generated, conversions to paid, languages used.

## GEOBoost
- Target persona: SMBs worried about AI visibility and website clarity.
- Message: AI visibility score + site audit; free audit.
- Conversion focus: Free audit, then upsell ongoing checks and improvements.
- KPIs: audits run, upgrades to paid plans, repeat usage.
