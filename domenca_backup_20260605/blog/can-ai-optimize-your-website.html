How We Used AI to Rank #1 on Google in 2 Weeks

Our website was invisible on Google. We used AI to audit, fix, and optimize it — and hit #1 in two weeks. Here's the exact playbook.

Everyone's talking about AI replacing jobs. But what about AI doing jobs — specifically, the job of getting your website to rank on Google?

We decided to find out. Not on a client's site. On our own.

hd-webdesign.si — our agency website — was invisible on Google. Not page 2. Not page 5. Nowhere. No structured data. No meta descriptions. Thin content. Missing schema markup. You name it, we didn't have it.

So we let AI take over the optimization. Two weeks later, we hit #1 on Google for our brand search.

This isn't theory. This is what actually happened, step by step, with real numbers.

The Starting Point: Zero Visibility

Before we started, our site scored 38/100 on our own audit tool. For a web design agency, that's like a mechanic driving a car with no wheels.

Here's what was wrong:

  • ❌ No meta descriptions on most pages — Google had no context for what we do
  • ❌ No structured data — search engines couldn't identify our business type, location, or services
  • ❌ No Open Graph tags — links shared on social media looked broken and unprofessional
  • ❌ No About page — no entity information for Google to connect our brand to
  • ❌ No blog content — zero topical authority, nothing to signal expertise
  • ❌ No sitemap.xml — Google didn't even know which pages existed
  • ❌ Thin pages — most pages had under 200 words of actual content

We weren't just unoptimized. We were invisible.

Step 1: The AI Audit (15 Minutes)

We ran the site through our combined audit tool, which simultaneously checks 5 categories:

  • SEO — titles, meta descriptions, H1 tags, content length, structured data
  • Security — HTTPS headers, mixed content, CSP, X-Frame-Options
  • Performance — page size, HTTP requests, compression
  • Accessibility — alt text, form labels, heading hierarchy
  • Cookies & Privacy — GDPR compliance, cookie banners

The AI identified 23 specific issues. Not vague advice like "improve your SEO." Concrete, actionable items with exact locations:

  • "Meta description missing on /boost-suite/ — add 150-160 character description"
  • "No H1 tag on homepage — add one that includes your primary keyword"
  • "4 images missing alt text — add descriptive alt text for accessibility and SEO"
  • "No Schema.org structured data — add Organization + LocalBusiness markup"
  • "Missing sitemap.xml — create and submit to Google Search Console"

15 minutes. 23 issues. Clear fixes for each one.

Step 2: Structured Data — The Game Changer

If we could only fix one thing, this would be it. Structured data (Schema.org markup) is code that tells search engines and AI tools exactly what your business is, what you do, and where you're located.

Most websites don't have it. We added 5 types:

  • Organization schema — company name, logo, social profiles, contact info
  • LocalBusiness schema — address, service area, opening hours
  • Article schema — for every blog post (author, date, headline)
  • FAQ schema — for common questions on service pages
  • Product schema — for our SaaS tools (BoostSuite, MenuBoost)

This single change had the biggest impact. Google went from "I have no idea what this site is about" to "This is a web design agency in Slovenia that offers specific AI-powered services." The shift was dramatic.

Step 3: Meta Tags & On-Page SEO (2 Hours)

AI generated optimized meta titles and descriptions for every page. The formula is simple:

  • Title: Primary keyword + value proposition + location (50-60 chars)
  • Description: What you do + who it's for + call to action (150-160 chars)
  • H1 tags: One per page, clear and keyword-rich

Before:

<title>HD Web Design</title>

(No meta description at all)

After:

<title>Web Design Agency Slovenia | AI-Powered Digital Solutions</title>

<meta name="description" content="Slovenian web design agency building AI-powered SaaS tools. Web design, SEO, GEO optimization. Free audits available.">

The difference is night and day. Google now knows exactly what each page is about and can match it to relevant searches.

Step 4: Content That Ranks (Ongoing)

Google rewards websites that demonstrate topical authority — publishing content that proves you genuinely know your stuff.

We added:

  • An About page with full entity information — who we are, what we do, our expertise, our location
  • Blog posts targeting specific questions our audience actually asks
  • Detailed service pages — not just bullet points, but real explanations of what we build and why
  • Case studies showing real results with real numbers (like this article)

AI helped draft the initial versions. A human refined them, added real experience, and removed anything that sounded generic. The combination is more powerful than either alone.

Step 5: GEO — Optimizing for AI Search

SEO gets you on Google. GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) gets you cited by ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity.

This is the part most businesses miss. When someone asks ChatGPT "What web design agencies are in Slovenia?" — you want to be in the answer. That requires different optimization than traditional SEO.

We optimized for AI search by:

  • Adding clear, factual statements that AI can extract and cite
  • Structuring content in Q&A format (with FAQ schema markup)
  • Using precise language — no vague marketing fluff that AI can't parse
  • Including specific data points (scores, numbers, dates) that AI tools love to reference

What AI Did vs. What Humans Did

Here's the honest breakdown:

AI handled (the execution):

  • Running comprehensive audits across 5 categories simultaneously
  • Identifying 23 specific issues with exact page locations
  • Generating optimized meta titles and descriptions for every page
  • Creating structured data (JSON-LD schema markup) for all content types
  • Drafting blog post outlines and initial content versions
  • Suggesting internal linking structure and content hierarchy

Humans handled (the strategy):

  • Deciding which keywords to target and which pages to prioritize
  • Maintaining brand voice — ensuring content sounds human, not robotic
  • Quality control — fact-checking, accuracy, removing AI hallucinations
  • Deployment — actually implementing changes on the live site
  • Business decisions — what services to highlight, how to position against competitors

The lesson: AI is the best employee you'll ever have — fast, tireless, and incredibly detailed. But it still needs a manager who understands the business.

Can You Do This Yourself?

Absolutely. Here's the playbook:

1. Audit your site first. Don't guess — measure. Use our free BoostSuite tool to get a complete audit in 10 seconds. No signup, no credit card.

2. Fix structured data first. This has the highest ROI of any single change. Add Organization, LocalBusiness, and Article schemas. If you sell products, add Product schema too.

3. Write proper meta tags. Every page needs a unique title (50-60 chars) and meta description (150-160 chars). Include your primary keyword and location.

4. Add real content. One blog post per week is enough. Write about what your customers actually ask you. Answer their questions thoroughly.

5. Don't ignore GEO. AI search is growing fast. Structure your content so ChatGPT and Perplexity can extract and cite it. FAQ format works incredibly well.

6. Measure and iterate. Re-run your audit monthly. Track your rankings. Adjust what isn't working.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can AI really optimize a website for SEO?

Yes. AI can audit your site, identify technical issues, generate optimized content, create structured data, and even write meta descriptions. The key is using AI as a tool to execute — not as a replacement for strategy.

How long does it take to see results?

We saw #1 ranking within 2 weeks. Technical fixes (structured data, meta tags) can show results in days. Content improvements take 2-4 weeks for Google to re-crawl and re-rank.

What is GEO optimization and why does it matter?

GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) optimizes your website to be cited by AI tools like ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity. As more people use AI search instead of Google, GEO ensures your business stays visible.
