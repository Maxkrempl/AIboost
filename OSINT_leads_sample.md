# OSINT Leads Sample - How to Use

## Overview
This file contains **20 qualified leads** across your 3 ICP segments:
- **SEO/Digital Marketing Agencies** (9 leads)
- **E-commerce Businesses** (6 leads: 4 Shopify + 1 Etsy + 1 aggregated)
- **Hospitality/Restaurants** (5 leads)

## Lead Quality Signals
Each lead was evaluated for:
- **Website presence** (all have functional websites)
- **Industry fit** (matches your app portfolio)
- **Company size** (1-500 employees; focus on small/mid)
- **Geography** (EU focus: UK, NL, DE, SI, AT, RO, PL, FR, Serbia)
- **WhyFit** (specific pain point or opportunity for your apps)

## How to Use These Leads

### 1. **Prioritization**
Start with:
- **House of Growth, Omnius, Exceed** — Boutique SEO agencies with founder involvement (warm outreach potential)
- **Gripped, Dapper** — Mid-size agencies with clear contact info
- **Gostilna Krištof, Gostilna AS** — Michelin-listed restaurants (high-quality signal)

### 2. **Outreach Strategy**
- **SEO/Marketing agencies**: Position SEOBooster + GEOBoost as tools they can use for clients or recommend
- **E-commerce (Shopify/Etsy)**: Direct pitch for EtsyBooster (Etsy sellers) or SEOBooster (Shopify stores needing SEO)
- **Restaurants**: MenuBoost + GEOBoost combo (menu descriptions + AI visibility)

### 3. **Contact Channels**
- **Email first**: Use contact pages or direct emails (hello@dapper.agency, hello@gripped.io)
- **LinkedIn warm-up**: Engage with their content before pitching
- **Phone (selective)**: Use for restaurants (they're less digital-first)

### 4. **Personalization Points**
Each lead has a **WhyFit** column — use that as your hook:
- _"Saw you're working with B2B SaaS clients — SEOBooster could be a great tool for your team to audit client sites in seconds."_
- _"Your Shopify store has great design — have you optimized product SEO yet?"_
- _"Michelin-listed restaurants deserve AI-optimized menu descriptions. MenuBoost can help."_

## Next Steps
1. Import CSV into your CRM or Google Sheets
2. Pick your top 5 leads
3. Draft personalized outreach (use templates in `Outreach_templates.md`)
4. Track responses and iterate
