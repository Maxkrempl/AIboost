GEO for Agencies: How to Sell AI Visibility Services to Your Clients

Your clients are asking you for SEO. But here's the truth: SEO is becoming a commodity. Every agency offers it. Prices are dropping. Margins are shrinking.

There's a new service that almost no agencies offer yet — and your clients desperately need it: GEO (Generative Engine Optimization).

This guide shows you how to add GEO as a revenue stream, what to charge, and how to pitch it to clients who don't even know they need it.

What Is GEO and Why Should Agencies Care?

GEO is the practice of making businesses visible to AI assistants — ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews. When someone asks an AI "best web agency in Vienna," GEO determines who gets recommended.

Why this matters for agencies:

  • 40%+ of product searches now start with AI assistants (not Google)
  • Most businesses have zero AI visibility — they're invisible to ChatGPT
  • Almost no agencies offer this service — first-mover advantage
  • It's technical work — clients can't DIY it (unlike basic SEO)

💡 The opportunity: Your client's website might rank #3 on Google — but be completely invisible to ChatGPT. That's a GEO problem, and you can fix it for them.

The 4 GEO Services You Can Sell

1. GEO Audit (Entry Point — Free or Low-Cost)

Run an AI visibility check on the client's website. Show them their score, what's missing, and why AI can't find them. This is your foot in the door.

What you deliver:

  • AI Visibility Score (0-100)
  • Technical checklist (Schema.org, llms.txt, FAQ, OpenGraph)
  • Competitor comparison (who AI recommends instead)

Price: Free (as lead magnet) or €50-100 one-time

2. Schema.org Implementation (Core Service)

Implement structured data markup on the client's website. This is the foundation of AI visibility — and most businesses don't have it.

What you deliver:

  • Organization, LocalBusiness, Service, FAQ schemas
  • Validation and testing
  • Documentation for the client

Price: €300-800 one-time (depending on complexity)

3. llms.txt + AI Content Optimization (Premium Service)

Create llms.txt files, optimize content for AI readability, and ensure the client's business information is consistent across directories.

What you deliver:

  • llms.txt creation and optimization
  • Content restructuring for AI readability
  • Directory listing consistency check

Price: €500-1,500 one-time

4. Ongoing GEO Monitoring (Retainer)

Monthly monitoring of AI visibility, new competitor tracking, and continuous optimization. This is your recurring revenue.

What you deliver:

  • Monthly AI visibility reports
  • Competitor monitoring
  • New FAQ content based on AI search trends
  • White-label reports with your agency branding

Price: €200-500/month retainer

How to Pitch GEO to Clients

The "Invisible to AI" Pitch

"Did you know that when someone asks ChatGPT 'best [your industry] in [your city]' — your business doesn't come up? I ran an AI visibility check on your website and found 4 critical issues preventing AI assistants from recommending you. Can I show you what I found?"

The Competitor Angle

"I checked which businesses ChatGPT recommends for [industry] in [city]. Your competitor [X] shows up, but you don't. Here's why — and here's how to fix it."

The ROI Pitch

"40% of searches now start with AI assistants. If your website isn't optimized for AI, you're invisible to nearly half your potential customers. GEO optimization costs €500 once and puts you in front of every AI search."

FAQ

Do I need to be a technical expert to sell GEO?

No. GEO implementation is mostly copy-paste (Schema.org templates, llms.txt files). Tools like BoostSuite automate the hard parts. You need to understand the concepts, not write code from scratch.

Will clients understand why they need GEO?

Not initially. That's why the free GEO audit works so well — it shows them the problem in concrete terms. "ChatGPT recommends your competitor but not you" is a powerful wake-up call.

How is this different from SEO?

SEO helps you rank on Google. GEO helps you get recommended by AI assistants. They complement each other — but GEO is newer, less competitive, and clients are starting to ask about it.

Can I bundle GEO with my existing SEO services?

Absolutely. GEO is a natural add-on to SEO. Offer it as a "AI Visibility Upgrade" to your existing SEO clients. Most will say yes because they're already paying you for search optimization.
