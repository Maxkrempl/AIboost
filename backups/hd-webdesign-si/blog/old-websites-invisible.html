Old Websites Are Becoming Invisible — Here's Why (and What to Do)

Your 2018 website is losing visitors every month. Google, ChatGPT, and Perplexity are burying outdated sites. Here's how to tell — and how to fix it.

June 5, 2026 · 7 min read

🇬🇧 EN 🇸🇮 SL

Three years ago, your website worked fine. It loaded, it looked decent on a laptop, and Google found it when people searched for your business.

Today, it's a different story. And if you haven't checked your analytics lately, you might not even notice what's happening.

Your website is becoming invisible.

Not because it's "down." Not because you did anything wrong. But because the rules changed — and your site didn't keep up.

53% of mobile visitors leave sites that take over 3 seconds to load
91% of websites now get less organic traffic than in 2023
0.8s is now Google's threshold for "fast" — most old sites take 3-5s

The Three Forces Burying Old Websites

It's not just one thing. Three separate shifts are happening simultaneously, and together they're creating a perfect storm for outdated sites.

1. Google's Core Web Vitals — The Speed Police

Since 2021, Google has been using Core Web Vitals as a ranking factor. These measure three things:

  • Largest Contentful Paint (LCP) — how fast your main content loads. Target: under 2.5 seconds.
  • Interaction to Next Paint (INP) — how responsive your page is when users click things. Target: under 200ms.
  • Cumulative Layout Shift (CLS) — how much the page jumps around while loading. Target: under 0.1.

A website built in 2018 with WordPress and 15 plugins? It probably fails all three. And in 2026, Google doesn't just rank you lower — it actively penalizes slow sites.

The uncomfortable truth: Google cares more about your site's performance than your site's content. A beautifully written page that loads in 5 seconds will lose to a mediocre page that loads in 1 second.

2. Mobile-First Indexing — Your Desktop Site Doesn't Matter

Google now indexes the mobile version of your site as the primary version. If your site was designed for desktop and "kind of works" on mobile — that's what Google sees.

Check these red flags:

  • Text too small to read on a phone
  • Buttons too close together (fat finger problem)
  • Horizontal scrolling required
  • Pop-ups that cover the entire screen
  • Images that aren't responsive

Old themes and templates from 2016-2019 often have "responsive" in their description, but the reality is a site that barely works on a 5-inch screen. Google notices.

3. AI Search Engines — The New Gatekeepers

This is the big one that most business owners don't know about yet.

When someone asks ChatGPT, Gemini, or Perplexity "What's the best web developer in Ljubljana?" or "Where should I eat in Bled?" — these AI engines don't just list websites. They summarize them, recommend them, and rank them based on:

  • Site speed and technical quality — slow, outdated sites get ignored
  • Structured data — does your site speak the language AI understands?
  • Content freshness — when was the last time you updated anything?
  • Mobile experience — AI assumes mobile-first because that's what users do
  • Schema markup — can AI parse your business info, reviews, and services?

A site built in 2018 with no schema, no structured data, and slow loading times is invisible to AI search. Not penalized — literally invisible. The AI skips it entirely.

This is called GEO (Generative Engine Optimization), and it's the biggest shift in search since Google itself launched. Your old website wasn't built for this world.

5 Signs Your Website Is Becoming Invisible

You don't need to be a developer to spot these. Check your site on your phone right now:

❌ It loads slowly on mobile — Open your site on your phone on 4G (not WiFi). If it takes more than 3 seconds, you're losing 53% of visitors before they see your content.

❌ It looks the same as it did 3+ years ago — Design trends evolve. If your site has the same layout from 2021, it signals to visitors (and Google) that your business isn't active.

❌ Google Analytics shows declining traffic — Compare your organic traffic from 2023 to now. If it's dropped 20-40%, Google is sending visitors elsewhere.

❌ No chatbot or AI assistant — In 2026, customers expect instant answers. A contact form that says "we'll get back to you in 24-48 hours" is a lost customer.

❌ You're not appearing in AI search results — Ask ChatGPT or Perplexity about your business category in your city. If you're not mentioned, AI doesn't know you exist.

What Happens When You Do Nothing

Let's be direct. If you ignore this:

  • Month 1-6: Traffic drops 10-20%. You might not notice because it's gradual.
  • Month 6-12: Competitors with modern sites outrank you consistently. Your "page 1" rankings slip to page 2.
  • Year 2: AI search engines skip your site entirely. New customers can't find you through ChatGPT or Google's AI Overviews.
  • Year 3: Your website is effectively a digital business card that only people who already know your name can find.

The businesses that thrive aren't the ones with the best products — they're the ones whose websites actually get found.

What a Modern Website Looks Like in 2026

A website built today should:

  • Load under 2 seconds on mobile (not 3-5 seconds)
  • Score 90+ on Google PageSpeed Insights
  • Have structured data so AI engines can understand your business
  • Include an AI chatbot that answers questions 24/7
  • Be mobile-first — designed for phones, not adapted from desktop
  • Use modern technology — no outdated plugins, no bloated themes
  • Have built-in SEO — proper meta tags, schema, sitemap, and fast hosting

This isn't luxury. This is the minimum to stay visible.
