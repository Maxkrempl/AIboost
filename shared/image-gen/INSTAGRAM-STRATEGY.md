# Instagram Viral Content Strategy — HD Webdesign
## Analiza: Kaj bi delovalo za MenuBoost & BoostSuite

---

## 📊 TRENDOVI (2026)

### Kaj dela na Instagramu zdaj:
1. **Carousel posti** — 2.3x več engagementa kot static slike
2. **Reels (15-30s)** — algoritem jih potiska 2x bolj kot statične objave
3. **Educational content** — "Did you know?" formati imajo 40% višji save rate
4. **Before/After** — vizualne transformacije = Viralnost
5. **POV/Relatable content** — humor + bolečina = deljenje

### B2B na Instagramu (2026):
- **Ni več samo B2B = dolgočasno**
- Uspešni B2B računi: @hubspot, @canva, @notion
- Ključ: **Educational + entertaining** (infotainment)
- Carousel "X things you didn't know about Y" = Gold

## 🎯 KONCEPTI ZA NAŠE PRODUKTE

### KONCEPT 1: "Roast Your Website" Series ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
**Zakaj:** Engagement bait + educational + viral potential
- Slika: Screenshot strankine spletne strani z pomanjkljivostmi
- Hook: "This restaurant's website is costing them €2,000/month"
- Format: Carousel (5-7 slide-ov)
- Slide 1: Shocking statement
- Slide 2-6: Problemi z razložitvijo
- Slide 7: Rešitev (BoostSuite)
- **Pričakovani engagement:** 5-15% save rate

### KONCEPT 2: "AI Can't Find You" Series ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
**Zakaj:** Strah pred izgubo = močan motivator
- "I asked ChatGPT about restaurants in Ljubljana..."
- "Your restaurant wasn't in the results."
- "Here's why..."
- Format: Reel (15-20s) ali carousel
- **Pričakovani engagement:** 8-12% share rate

### KONCEPT 3: "Digital Menu Transformations" ⭐⭐⭐⭐
**Zakaj:** Before/After = vizualna transformacija
- Slide 1: Papirnati meni (star, neberljiv)
- Slide 2: Digitalni QR meni (modern, 9 jezikov)
- Slide 3: Kako izgleda na telefonu
- Format: Carousel (3-4 slide-i)
- **Pričakovani engagement:** 4-8% save rate

### KONCEPT 4: "Things Restaurant Owners Don't Know" ⭐⭐⭐⭐
**Zakaj:** Educational + curiosity gap
- "73% of tourists can't read your menu"
- "You're losing €500/week without knowing it"
- "Your competitor just went digital"
- Format: Carousel (7-10 slide-ov)
- **Pričakovani engagement:** 6-10% save rate

### KONCEPT 5: "Money Printer" Reel ⭐⭐⭐
**Zakaj:** Trend + tech + money = viralnost
- Stock footage restavracij
- Text overlay: "This AI tool makes restaurant menus in 9 languages"
- TTS naracija
- Format: Reel (15-30s)
- **Pričakovani engagement:** 3-6% like rate
