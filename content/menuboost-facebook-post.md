Živjo! 👋

Idealno za turistične kmetije, gostilne, restavracije ki želijo privabiti tuje goste. Preizkusite in mi povejte, kaj mislite! 😊

Ideale per trattorie, ristoranti, agriturismi che vogliono attirare turisti straniere. Provatelo e fatemi sapere! 😊

## Usage Tips

- Post in groups with 500+ members
- Best times: Tuesday-Thursday, 9-11 AM or 7-9 PM
- Don't post the same message in multiple groups on the same day
- Respond to comments quickly — engagement boosts visibility
- If admins ask, say you're a restaurant owner who built this tool
