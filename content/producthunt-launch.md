# Product Hunt Launch Checklist — MenuBoost

## Pre-Launch (1 week before)

- [ ] Create Product Hunt maker account (if not exists)
- [ ] Prepare product page:
  - [ ] Tagline: "AI menu descriptions for restaurants in 6 languages — in 10 seconds"
  - [ ] Description (short + long)
  - [ ] Images: 3-5 screenshots of the tool in action
  - [ ] Video: 30-second demo (screen recording)
  - [ ] Logo: 240x240px
- [ ] Prepare maker comment (first comment on launch day)
- [ ] Build a "launch team" — 10-20 people who will upvote + comment on launch day
- [ ] Schedule launch for **Tuesday or Wednesday** (best days for PH)

## Launch Day

- [ ] Submit at 12:01 AM Pacific Time (PT)
- [ ] Post maker comment immediately
- [ ] Share on LinkedIn, Twitter, Facebook
- [ ] Email your list (if any)
- [ ] Post in relevant communities (r/SideProject, Hacker News)
- [ ] Respond to every comment within 1 hour

## Maker Comment Template

"Hey Product Hunt! 👋

I'm Darko, the maker of MenuBoost.

I built this because I was tired of seeing restaurants struggle with menu translations. Tourists can't read your menu → they don't order → you lose revenue.

MenuBoost uses AI to generate appetizing menu descriptions in 6 languages (Slovenian, Croatian, English, German, Italian, Serbian) in under 10 seconds.

Try it free (3 descriptions, no signup): https://menuboostai.netlify.app

Pro plan: €19/month for unlimited translations.

I'd love your feedback — what would make this more useful for restaurants?"

## Post-Launch

- [ ] Thank everyone who supported
- [ ] Write a "lessons learned" post
- [ ] Collect feedback and iterate
- [ ] Update the product page with PH badge

## Best Time to Launch

- **Day:** Tuesday or Wednesday
- **Time:** 12:01 AM PT (9:01 AM CET)
- **Avoid:** Monday (too early in week), Friday (people are leaving)
