## Post 3: GEO vs SEO

GEO ≠ SEO. Here's the difference:

SEO = optimizing for Google's algorithm
GEO = optimizing for AI's citation patterns

In SEO, you rank #1 and get traffic.
In GEO, you're either cited or invisible. There's no "position 5" in a ChatGPT response.

What affects GEO citations:
• Entity authority (Wikipedia, Wikidata mentions)
• Structured data (schema markup)
• Content that answers questions directly
• Freshness and recency signals
• Domain trust across multiple sources

I built a free tool to check any business's GEO visibility: https://boostsuite.netlify.app
