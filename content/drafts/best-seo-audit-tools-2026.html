Detailed Reviews

1. SEOptimer $29/mo

SEOptimer is one of the most popular SEO audit tools, especially for agencies that need white-label PDF reports. It offers a clean interface and embeddable audit widgets.

✅ Pros

  • Beautiful white-label PDF reports
  • Embeddable audit widget for lead generation
  • SEO + GEO checks
  • 15 language support

❌ Cons

  • Limited free tier
  • No ad copy or listing optimization
  • Higher tiers get expensive ($59/mo for embedding)
Verdict: Great for agencies that need white-label reports. The embeddable widget is excellent for lead generation.
2. Ubersuggest $5/mo

Neil Patel's Ubersuggest is the most affordable option for beginners. It covers basic SEO auditing along with keyword research and backlink analysis.

✅ Pros

  • Very affordable ($5/mo individual)
  • Keyword research included
  • Backlink analysis
  • Neil Patel's content/marketing resources

❌ Cons

  • Basic audit compared to dedicated tools
  • No white-label reports
  • Data accuracy questions
Verdict: Best budget option for freelancers who need basic SEO insights without breaking the bank.
3. Ahrefs €37/mo

Ahrefs is the gold standard for backlink analysis and technical SEO. It's powerful but expensive, making it better suited for established agencies.

✅ Pros

  • Best backlink database in the industry
  • Comprehensive technical SEO audits
  • Content explorer and keyword explorer
  • AI-powered features

❌ Cons

  • Expensive (€37-234/mo)
  • Steep learning curve
  • No free tier
Verdict: The best tool for serious SEO professionals, but the price puts it out of reach for freelancers just starting out.
4. BoostSuite €19/mo

BoostSuite is a newer entrant that takes a different approach: instead of just SEO auditing, it combines 4 marketing tools in one platform — SEO Audit, GEO Check (AI Visibility), Ad Copy Generator, and Listing Optimizer.

✅ Pros

  • 4 tools in one (SEO + GEO + Ad Copy + Listings)
  • AI-powered GEO visibility check (unique!)
  • Most affordable all-in-one option
  • 10 languages including Slavic languages
  • White-label reports on Agency plan

❌ Cons

  • Newer tool, smaller user base
  • Free tier limited to 3 uses/day
  • No backlink analysis yet
Verdict: Best value for agencies who want to replace multiple subscriptions with one tool. The GEO check is unique in the market.
5. Screaming Frog Free (500 URLs)

Screaming Frog is a desktop-based crawler that's essential for technical SEO. It's not a cloud tool — you run it locally on your machine.

✅ Pros

  • Free up to 500 URLs
  • Deep technical crawl analysis
  • Industry standard for technical SEO
  • Desktop app = no ongoing subscription

❌ Cons

  • Desktop only (no cloud/web version)
  • Steep learning curve
  • No AI features or content suggestions
Verdict: Essential for technical SEO audits, but not a complete marketing toolkit. Best used alongside other tools.

What to Look for in an SEO Audit Tool

When choosing an SEO audit tool, consider these factors:

  • Depth of analysis: Does it cover on-page, technical, and off-page SEO?
  • Reporting: Can you export professional reports for clients?
  • Pricing: Does the pricing scale with your business?
  • Extra features: Does it include keyword research, backlink analysis, or content optimization?
  • AI features: Does it offer AI-powered recommendations or GEO visibility checks?

Frequently Asked Questions

What is an SEO audit tool?

An SEO audit tool analyzes a website's search engine optimization — checking factors like title tags, meta descriptions, heading structure, image alt text, page speed, and more. It provides a score and actionable recommendations to improve rankings.

How much does an SEO audit tool cost?

SEO audit tools range from free (SmallSEOTools, Screaming Frog free tier) to $5-29/mo for basic tools (Ubersuggest, BoostSuite) to $39-234/mo for enterprise platforms (Ahrefs, SEMrush, Moz).

Can I do an SEO audit for free?

Yes! Tools like BoostSuite (3 free audits/day), SEOptimer (limited free audit), and SmallSEOTools offer free SEO audits. For deeper analysis, you'll need a paid plan.

What is GEO (Generative Engine Optimization)?

GEO is the practice of optimizing your website to appear in AI chatbot responses (ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity). It's a new field — only a few tools like BoostSuite and SEOptimer currently offer GEO visibility checks.

Which SEO tool is best for agencies?

For agencies, look for: white-label reports, client-ready PDFs, multiple tool types in one platform, and team pricing. BoostSuite (€49/mo agency plan) and SEOptimer ($59/mo embedding plan) are both designed specifically for agency workflows.
