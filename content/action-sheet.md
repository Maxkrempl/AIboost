## 💡 Key Insight

**Cold email is the HARDEST channel.** The easiest paying users come from:
1. People who find you via search (blog/SEO)
2. People who see your content on social media
3. People who hear about you from a trusted source (partnerships)

Cold email should be ONE channel, not the ONLY channel. Focus on content + social + partnerships alongside outreach.
