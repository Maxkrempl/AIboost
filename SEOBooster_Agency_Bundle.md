SEOBooster Agency Bundle — Reports for Agencies (Target: SEO/Digital-Marketing Agencies)

Overview
- SEOBooster is a free AI-powered SEO audit tool for website analysis (scores 0-100, titles, meta, keywords, content quality, links). This bundle packages ready-to-deliver audits for agencies to standardize client reporting, scale outreach, and white-label the experience.

Bundle Components
- Agency Audit Report Pack (3 report styles)
  - Executive Summary (1 page): site health snapshot, score, top fixes, quick wins, and estimated impact.
  - Standard Report (4-6 pages): detailed findings for title tags, meta descriptions, keyword usage, content quality, internal/external links, and a prioritized action list.
  - Comprehensive Report (10+ pages): full audit with screenshots, historical context, benchmarking against competitors, and an implementation roadmap.
  - All reports include: client-facing language, clear next steps, and optional ROI framing.
- White-label Branding Kit
  - Branding-ready templates with placeholders for agency logo, colors, fonts, and contact details.
  - Export formats: PDF and HTML-ready reports; simple copy/paste HTML for client portals.
- Delivery & Scheduling Kit
  - Automated cadence options (e.g., monthly audits, on-demand audits).
  - Bulk run capability for multiple client sites.
  - Client portal-ready summaries that can be embedded or linked from agency systems.
- Licensing & Usage
  - Agency licensing model (internal rebranded use, client-delivery rights).
  - Per-seat or per-audit pricing options (to be modeled with the agency's needs).
- Outreach & Sales Playbook (for acquiring agency clients)
  - 5 warm outreach email templates tailored to agencies (cold, warm, and follow-up).
  - 3 LinkedIn messages and 2 phone-call scripts.
  - A simple value proposition framework: score, fixes, and potential improvements per month.
- Case Study & Social Proof Templates
  - 1-page case study template (problem, actions, results).
  - 2 social post templates for agency outreach.
- Multilingual Support
  - Reports configurable in 10 languages (English, Slovenian, Croatian, Serbian, German, Italian, French, Spanish, Dutch, Polish).

Usage Scenarios
- Agencies offering SEO audits as a service to SMBs across e-commerce, blogs, and service sites.
- White-labeled audit delivery to client portals or email.
- Scalable outreach using ready-made templates to accelerate client conversion.
