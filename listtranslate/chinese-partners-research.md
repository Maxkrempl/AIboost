## 2. Social Media Platforms Where Sellers Are Active

### Xiaohongshu (小红书 / RedNote)
- **Why:** 2.5亿+ monthly active users. Cross-border e-commerce is a hot topic. Many sellers share experiences and tools.
- **Search terms:** "跨境电商 工具", "亚马逊 listing翻译", "Shopee运营"
- **Strategy:** Create content about AI translation tools, share before/after examples of translated listings
- **Note:** Xiaohongshu is launching **RedShop** (跨境电商平台) in June 2026 — huge opportunity for cross-border tools

### Zhihu (知乎)
- **Why:** High-quality Q&A platform. Cross-border e-commerce topics have massive traffic.
- **Search terms:** "跨境电商 做什么平台好", "亚马逊 listing 优化", "翻译工具 推荐"
- **Strategy:** Answer questions about listing optimization, recommend ListTranslate as a solution
- **Key content:** "2026年跨境电商必备工具" type articles

### Douyin/TikTok (抖音)
- **Why:** Short video tutorials about cross-border e-commerce are extremely popular
- **Strategy:** Create short videos showing ListTranslate in action (before/after translation)
- **Search:** "跨境电商教程", "亚马逊运营技巧", "AI翻译工具"

### WeChat Official Accounts (微信公众号)

## 9. Outreach Strategy

### Phase 1: Foundation (Weeks 1-2)
1. **Create Chinese landing page** — ListTranslate needs a Chinese-language website or landing page
2. **Set up WeChat Official Account** — Chinese sellers primarily use WeChat
3. **Register on navigation sites** — AMZ123, 卖家之家, DNY123, TT123 tool directories
4. **Create accounts on forums** — 知无不言, 创蓝, 雨果

### Phase 2: Content Marketing (Weeks 3-6)
1. **Zhihu articles** — Write about AI listing translation, cross-border tool recommendations
2. **Forum posts** — Share genuine value on 知无不言 and 创蓝, mention ListTranslate where relevant
3. **Xiaohongshu content** — Short before/after translation demos
4. **Guest articles** — Pitch to 雨果网 and 亿恩网

### Phase 3: Partnership Outreach (Weeks 4-8)
1. **ERP integrations** — Reach out to 领星, 芒果店长, 积加, EasyBoss for API integration
2. **Training agencies** — Offer bulk Pro accounts to 亿丰, 拓扑, 黑马 students
3. **Navigation directories** — Get listed on all major tool directories
4. **Affiliate program** — Set up affiliate commissions for forum admins and training agencies

### Phase 4: Event Marketing (Ongoing)
1. **Attend CCEE** — 雨果跨境's major cross-border e-commerce exhibition
2. **Attend 卖家大会** — Various seller conferences throughout the year
3. **Host webinars** — "AI翻译如何提升跨境电商Listing转化率"

### Pricing Strategy for Chinese Market
- **Free tier:** 5 translations/month — get them hooked
- **Pro ¥99/month** — Position against manual translation costs (猪八戒 listings charge ¥50-200+ per listing)
- **Enterprise ¥299/month** — Bulk discounts for training agencies and ERP integrations

### Key Messaging (Chinese)
- "AI一键翻译，告别Listing翻译烦恼" (AI one-click translation, say goodbye to listing translation headaches)
- "中文→英/日/泰/马，4语种精准翻译" (Chinese → EN/JP/TH/MY, 4 languages precise translation)
- "比人工翻译快100倍，成本降低90%" (100x faster than manual translation, 90% cost reduction)
- "支持亚马逊、Shopee、TikTok Shop多平台" (Supports Amazon, Shopee, TikTok Shop multi-platform)
