1. **Use AI tools for the first draft** — I use ListTranslate (listtranslate.netlify.app) to generate optimized English listings from Chinese product descriptions. It's free for 5 listings/month.

2. **Have a native speaker review** — Once you have the AI draft, find a native English speaker on Fiverr ($10-20) to review and polish it.

3. **Iterate based on data** — After your listing is live, check your conversion rate. If it's below 10%, the listing needs work.

This approach costs me about $20/month for 50 listings, vs $200-500 per listing with a professional writer.
