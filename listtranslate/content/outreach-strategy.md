# ListTranslate — Chinese Seller Outreach Strategy

## Why This Works
Chinese Amazon sellers are EVERYWHERE in online communities. They don't need us to find their email — they're actively looking for tools to improve their English listings. We go to THEM.

## Target Communities (Where Chinese Sellers Hang Out)

### Facebook Groups (Highest Priority)
These groups have 10k-100k+ Chinese cross-border sellers:

1. **Amazon FBA Chinese Sellers** — Search "亚马逊卖家" or "Amazon FBA" in Facebook
2. **Cross-border E-commerce Community** — "跨境电商交流群"
3. **Shopee/Southeast Asia Sellers** — "Shopee卖家交流"
4. **Amazon Global Selling** — "亚马逊全球开店"

### Reddit
- r/AmazonSeller — English-speaking but many Chinese lurkers
- r/FulfillmentByAmazon — Same
- r/ecommerce — General but relevant

### Forums
- Amazon Seller Central Forums — Chinese sellers post here (we saw them in search results)
-知无不言 (Zhiwuyuyan) — Major Chinese Amazon seller forum
- 亿恩网 (Ennews) — Cross-border e-commerce news/community

### Quora
- "Chinese Amazon seller" related questions
- Cross-border e-commerce questions in Chinese

## Outreach Methods

### Method 1: Facebook Group Posts (FREE, Highest ROI)
Post helpful content in Chinese seller groups:
- "免费AI listing翻译工具推荐" (Free AI listing translation tool recommendation)
- Before/after examples
- Cost savings calculation
- No hard sell — position as fellow seller sharing a tool

### Method 2: Cold DM to Group Members
Find active members in groups → Send personalized DM in Chinese

### Method 3: Reddit/Forum Posts
Post genuine helpful content mentioning ListTranslate naturally

### Method 4: Direct Amazon Seller Messages
Through Amazon Seller Central buyer-seller messaging (limited but possible)

## Content to Create
1. Facebook group post templates (Chinese)
2. DM templates (Chinese)  
3. Reddit post templates (English, targeting Chinese sellers who read English)
4. Forum post templates

## Metrics
- Target: 50 DMs/day across platforms
- Expected response rate: 5-10%
- Goal: 100 trial signups in 30 days
