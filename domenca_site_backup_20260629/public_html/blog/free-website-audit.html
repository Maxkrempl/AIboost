Why Most Website Audits Are Useless

Most free audit tools give you a 50-page PDF full of technical jargon. You close it and never look at it again. That's not an audit — that's homework.

A good audit should answer three questions:

  1. What's broken? — Specific issues, not vague scores
  2. How bad is it? — Priority ranking (critical vs nice-to-have)
  3. How do I fix it? — Plain-English instructions

The BoostSuite free audit does exactly this. Each issue comes with a fix explanation that any website owner can follow — no developer needed.
