What is HD Web Design?

Published May 16, 2026 · 5 min read

HD Web Design (hd-webdesign.si) is a Slovenian brand and platform that builds AI-powered SaaS tools for businesses. Founded by developer Darko Herceg, HD Web Design creates practical software that automates real tasks — from generating restaurant menus to auditing SEO and creating ad copy.

The Problem HD Web Design Solves

Many businesses — especially restaurants, freelancers, and small agencies — spend hours on repetitive tasks: writing menu descriptions in multiple languages, analyzing SEO performance, creating ad copy, or optimizing product listings. HD Web Design's tools use AI to automate these tasks, saving time and improving quality.

The Products

🍽️ MenuBoost — Multilingual Menu AI

MenuBoost helps restaurants create professional menu descriptions in 6 languages: Slovenian, Croatian, English, German, Italian, and Serbian. A restaurant owner enters their dish name and basic description, and MenuBoost generates a compelling, culturally-appropriate description ready for print or web.

Who uses it: Tourist farms in Slovenia, restaurants in Croatia, coastal Italian restaurants, and hotels across the Adriatic region.

🔍 BoostSuite — Marketing Toolkit for Agencies

BoostSuite is a 4-in-1 AI marketing toolkit designed for agencies and freelancers:

  • SEO Audit — Analyze any URL for on-page SEO issues, get a score and actionable fixes.
  • GEO Check — Check business visibility across AI assistants and directories. See how likely AI is to recommend your business.
  • Ad Copy Generator — Generate converting ad copy for Google, Facebook, and Instagram.
  • Listing Optimizer — Optimize product listings for Etsy, Amazon, and Google Business.

Who uses it: Marketing agencies, SEO freelancers, e-commerce sellers, and businesses wanting to improve their online presence.

📢 AdBoost — AI Ad Copy

AdBoost is a focused tool for generating ad copy. Enter your product or service, target audience, and platform — get multiple ad variations in seconds.

Why Slovenia?

HD Web Design is based in Slovenia, a small EU country with a growing tech scene. Being based in Slovenia gives HD Web Design:

  • EU market access — GDPR-compliant, serving businesses across the European Union.
  • Multilingual focus — Deep understanding of European language needs, especially in the Adriatic region (Slovenia, Croatia, Italy).
  • Competitive pricing — European quality at accessible price points (€0–49/month).

Technology

All HD Web Design products are built with modern web technologies:

  • Frontend: Vanilla JavaScript, HTML5, CSS3
  • AI Engine: DeepSeek AI for content generation and analysis
  • Hosting: Domenca (hd-webdesign.si, custom subdomains)
  • Payments: Gumroad (simple subscription billing)
  • Email: Resend (transactional email)

Pricing

HD Web Design products are designed to be accessible:

  • Free tier: 3 uses per day, no signup required
  • Freelancer: €19/month — 200 uses, PDF reports, email support
  • Agency: €49/month — Unlimited uses, white-label reports, API access, team seats
