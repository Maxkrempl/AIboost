## Tips for All Templates
- **Personalize the first line** (mention something specific about them)
- **Keep it short** (under 100 words)
- **One clear CTA** (try the tool, book a call, or ask a question)
- **No attachments** (link to the tool instead)
- **Follow up after 3-5 days** if no response

---

**Pro Tip**: For agencies, offer a **free Pro account** or **affiliate deal** (10-20% commission on referrals). For restaurants, offer a free Pro trial in exchange for a testimonial.
